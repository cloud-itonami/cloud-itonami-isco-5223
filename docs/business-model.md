# Business Model: Independent Retail Floor Sales Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-5223`
- ISCO-08: `5223`
- Occupation: Shop Sales Assistants
- Social impact: local-jobs, customer-access, small-retail-viability

## Customer

- small retailers
- market stalls
- pop-up shops

## Offer

- shelf and price-tag checks
- customer advisory
- sales logging
- restock flagging
- reporting

## Revenue

- monthly floor-operations platform fee
- per-transaction fee
- reporting package

## Trust Controls

- no shelf-robot operation near customers without governor gate
- sales records are auditable, not editable
- price changes require sign-off
